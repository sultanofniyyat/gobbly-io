What kinds of rolling ball games are there?

Last updated 2026-11-17

Rolling ball games come in a few kinds: tilt mazes, downhill runners, physics puzzles and eat-and-grow games. Gobbly.io is an eat-and-grow ball game: you drag to roll a ball that eats food and toys and grows through sizes.

What are the main kinds?

KindWhat you do
Tilt mazeTilt the phone to guide a ball to a hole
RunnerSteer a ball down a track without falling off
Physics puzzleBuild or move parts so the ball reaches the goal
Eat and growRoll around, swallow things, get bigger (Gobbly.io)

How does the ball move in Gobbly.io?

Touch anywhere on the screen and drag: the ball rolls the way you drag. Lift your finger and it stops. You never tilt the phone. The ball grows as it eats, and the camera zooms out so you can still see around you.
