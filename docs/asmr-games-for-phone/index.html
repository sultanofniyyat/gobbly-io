What are ASMR games on mobile?

Last updated 2026-11-17

"ASMR games" are phone games built around soothing sounds and satisfying visuals: crunching, popping, slicing, cleaning or sorting. Gobbly.io is not a pure ASMR game, but it shares the feel: food pops into the ball with little sounds, "+1"s and light vibration.

What makes a game feel ASMR?

  • Crisp, repeated sounds (pops, crunches, clicks).
  • Smooth motion and a clean, tidy result.
  • Little pressure, so you can just watch and listen.

Does Gobbly.io have sound and vibration?

Yes. Eating plays small pops, the biggest bites make a deeper gulp, and the phone gives light vibration. You can turn sound, music and vibration on or off in Settings. Headphones make it nicer.
