Is Gobbly.io suitable for kids?

Last updated 2026-11-17

The game itself is simple and non-violent: a ball rolls around eating food and toys. It is rated 4+ on the App Store; the game is meant for players aged 13 and over. Parents should know about ads and purchases: There are optional reward videos you can choose to watch for extras such as more time, a heart or coins. From level 4, a short full-screen ad can also appear now and then between levels and when you come back to the game. Optional purchases are coin packs, booster bundles, a Gold Season Pass and a one-time Remove Ads. Every level can be beaten without buying anything.

Is there violence or scary content?

No. Food and toys disappear into the ball. Bombs give a cartoon "BOOM" and end the level; nothing is hurt.

Can kids talk to strangers in the game?

No. There is no chat and no messaging. Gobbly.io is a single-player game. There are no other balls in a level and no live opponents. The Weekly Contest and rankings are worked out on your phone, and there is no chat.

Can my child spend money?

Optional purchases are coin packs, booster bundles, a Gold Season Pass and a one-time Remove Ads. Every level can be beaten without buying anything. Purchases go through the App Store or Google Play, so you can require approval for every purchase: on iPhone with Screen Time (Content & Privacy Restrictions → iTunes & App Store Purchases) or Ask to Buy, and on Android in the Play Store settings (Authentication → Require authentication for purchases) or with Google Family Link.

What data does the game collect?

The game does not ask for an account, your name, your email, your contacts or your location. Two analytics services (GameAnalytics and Google Analytics for Firebase) and one ad service (Google AdMob) receive some device and game information, as the privacy policy explains. Full details: iPhone privacy policy and Android privacy policy.

Is it easy enough for young children?

The controls are: one finger, no reading needed to play. Later levels and the timer can be hard for young children.
