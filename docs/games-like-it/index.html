What are the best games where you eat things and grow?

Last updated 2026-11-17

The best-known eat-and-grow games are Katamari Damacy (roll a sticky ball that grows), Hole.io (a hole that swallows a city), Donut County (a story game about a growing hole) and Agar.io (cells that eat to grow). Gobbly.io mixes the rolling ball idea with short timed levels and a goal list.

Which eat-and-grow games are worth knowing?

GameWhat it is
Katamari Damacy (2004)The original rolling-ball game from Namco. Roll a sticky ball that picks up everything it touches. Console and PC.
Agar.io (2015)A browser and mobile game where you are a cell eating pellets and smaller players. Started the io style.
Hole.io (2018)A mobile game where you are a hole in a city, racing other holes in short timed rounds.
Donut County (2018)A story-driven game where you play a growing hole. Mobile, console and PC.
Gobbly.ioA rolling ball that eats food and toys in short timed levels with a goal list, boosters and events. Free on iPhone and Android.

Which one should I pick?

  • A story and a finished adventure: Donut County or Katamari Damacy.
  • Racing other players: Hole.io or Agar.io.
  • Short levels with a clear goal on your phone, alone and offline: Gobbly.io.
