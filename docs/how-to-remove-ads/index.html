How do you remove ads in Gobbly.io?

Last updated 2026-11-17

Tap REMOVE ADS on the ad break screen. It is a one-time purchase that switches off the ads between levels and when you open the game, for good. Buying anything else in the game switches them off too. Reward videos you choose to watch stay available.

What ads are there?

There are optional reward videos you can choose to watch for extras such as more time, a heart or coins. From level 4, a short full-screen ad can also appear now and then between levels and when you come back to the game.

I paid to remove ads but still see them. What do I do?

  1. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you used to buy.
  2. Open the game's Settings and tap Restore purchases.
  3. Close the game fully and open it again.
  4. Still not working? Email sultanofniyyat@gmail.com with the date of the purchase (on Android, the order number that starts with GPA.) and we will sort it out.

Are the reward videos optional?

Yes. Videos that give a reward, such as more time or a heart, only play when you tap to watch them.

I saw an inappropriate ad. How do I report it?

Ads come from Google's ad network, not from us, but we can block ad categories and advertisers. Email sultanofniyyat@gmail.com with what the ad showed and, if you can, a screenshot.
