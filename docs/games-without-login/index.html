Are there good games that need no login or account?

Last updated 2026-11-17

Yes. Many single-player games let you play without creating an account: you open the app and start. Gobbly.io is one of them: no account, no name, no email. The game does not ask for an account, your name, your email, your contacts or your location. Two analytics services (GameAnalytics and Google Analytics for Firebase) and one ad service (Google AdMob) receive some device and game information, as the privacy policy explains.

Why play games without an account?

  • No passwords to remember.
  • Less personal information shared.
  • You start playing in seconds.

What is the downside?

Without an account, moving progress to a new phone can be harder. Your progress is saved on your phone automatically. On Android it is also saved in the cloud with Google Play Games, so it comes back on a new phone signed in to the same Google account. On iPhone there is no cloud save yet, so deleting the app deletes your progress. Remove Ads comes back on any phone with Restore Purchases.

How do I check what a game collects?

Read the privacy section on the store page (App Store "App Privacy", Google Play "Data safety") and the game's privacy policy. Ours: iPhone and Android.
