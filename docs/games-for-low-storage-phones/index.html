What games work on phones with little storage?

Last updated 2026-11-17

For phones with little space, look for games under about 150 MB that do not download big extra packs after installing. Gobbly.io is about 90 MB and needs iOS 15 or newer on iPhone or Android 7.0 or newer.

How do I check a game's real size?

  • The store page shows the download size.
  • Some games download more after the first launch. Check your phone's storage settings after opening the game once.

How do I free up space?

  1. Delete apps you have not opened in months.
  2. Back up photos and videos to the cloud or a computer.
  3. Clear big chat downloads.

Does Gobbly.io run on older phones?

Yes. It changes its graphics to suit the phone: phones with less memory get simpler shadows to stay smooth.
