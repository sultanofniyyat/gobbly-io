Privacy policy

Gobbly.io has one privacy policy for each store. They say exactly what the game collects, which services receive it and why.

  • Privacy policy for iPhone
  • Privacy policy for Android

In short

The game does not ask for an account, your name, your email, your contacts or your location. Two analytics services (GameAnalytics and Google Analytics for Firebase) and one ad service (Google AdMob) receive some device and game information, as the privacy policy explains. We do not sell your information. In the EU, UK and Switzerland you are asked before personalised ads are shown.

This website

This website has no cookies, no analytics and no forms. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which keeps basic server logs (such as IP addresses) under GitHub's own privacy policy.
