What can the ball eat, and why can't I eat that yet?

Last updated 2026-11-17

The ball can eat anything whose size is the same as the ball's size or smaller. If something will not go in, the ball is not big enough yet: eat smaller things nearby, grow a size, and come back.

How does the size rule work?

Every item belongs to a size, from Size 1 (cherries, cookies, small sweets) up to the biggest things on the map. The ball starts every level at Size 1. Everything you eat fills the ring around it, and when the ring is full the ball grows to the next size and can eat bigger things. Things that are too big for you act like walls: the ball slides along them.

How do I know when I can eat something new?

When the ball grows, the game shows which new things you can now eat. On early levels a small red ring also flashes when you bump into something that is still too big.

What are the piles and stacks?

Many levels have tall stacks and big heaps of food. The ball eats them from the bottom and the rest slides down into it, so a whole pile pours in while you hold still next to it.

What should I never eat?

Bombs. Some maps have bombs. If the ball eats a bomb, the level ends, unless a Shield is protecting you. You can then pay coins to carry on, or give up.

Why did my ball stop growing?

Usually because the things around you are already eaten, or still too big. Head for an area full of food you can eat. The Compass booster points you to the goal items you still need.
