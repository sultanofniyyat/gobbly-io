What games are like Hole.io?

Last updated 2026-11-17

Games like Hole.io are eat-and-grow games: you swallow things and grow until you can swallow the biggest objects. Some keep the online race against other holes; others turn it into levels. Gobbly.io is a level-based one where you play as a rolling ball.

What do Hole.io fans usually love?

  • The moment you are finally big enough for a building.
  • Short timed rounds.
  • One-finger control.

Is Gobbly.io like Hole.io?

It has the same growing feeling, but you are a ball, every level has a goal list, and there are no other players. More detail: how Gobbly.io differs from Hole.io.
