What are good games you can play with one hand?

Last updated 2026-11-17

One-hand games are played in portrait mode with one finger: tapping, swiping or dragging. Good kinds are eat-and-grow games, runners, merge games and many puzzle games. Gobbly.io is fully one-finger: Touch anywhere on the screen and drag: the ball rolls the way you drag. Lift your finger and it stops.

When is a one-hand game useful?

  • Standing on a bus or train, holding on with the other hand.
  • Lying down, holding a drink, or holding a baby.
  • Playing with the thumb of the hand holding the phone.

What should I check?

Portrait mode, no buttons in the far corners and no need for two fingers at once.

Does Gobbly.io work one-handed?

Yes. It is played upright, you can drag from anywhere on the screen, and the only buttons are the booster bar at the bottom and pause at the top.
