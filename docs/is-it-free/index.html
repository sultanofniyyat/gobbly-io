Is Gobbly.io free?

Last updated 2026-11-17

Gobbly.io is free to download and free to play. There are optional reward videos you can choose to watch for extras such as more time, a heart or coins. From level 4, a short full-screen ad can also appear now and then between levels and when you come back to the game. Optional purchases are coin packs, booster bundles, a Gold Season Pass and a one-time Remove Ads. Every level can be beaten without buying anything.

Do I have to pay to win levels?

No. Every level can be beaten without boosters or purchases. Boosters make a tight level easier, and you also earn them as rewards for playing.

What can you buy?

Optional purchases are coin packs, booster bundles, a Gold Season Pass and a one-time Remove Ads. Every level can be beaten without buying anything. Prices are shown in the game in your own currency before you buy.

How do ads work in the game?

There are optional reward videos you can choose to watch for extras such as more time, a heart or coins. From level 4, a short full-screen ad can also appear now and then between levels and when you come back to the game.

Can I get rid of the ads?

Tap REMOVE ADS on the ad break screen. It is a one-time purchase that switches off the ads between levels and when you open the game, for good. Buying anything else in the game switches them off too. Reward videos you choose to watch stay available. More details: how to remove ads.

Why is the game free?

Gobbly.io is made by a small independent developer, Sultan of Niyyat. Ads and optional purchases pay for development and updates, so anyone can play without paying up front.
