What are good games for flights and long trips?

Last updated 2026-11-17

The best travel games work in airplane mode, do not drain the battery fast and come in short pieces you can pause between. Download them at home on Wi-Fi and test them in airplane mode before you leave. Gobbly.io works fully offline.

Checklist before you travel

  1. Install the games on Wi-Fi at home.
  2. Open each one once, then try it in airplane mode.
  3. Charge the phone and bring a power bank.
  4. Lower screen brightness to save battery.

Which kinds of games are best for travel?

Level-based puzzle and casual games, card games, and story games you have already downloaded. Avoid games that need online matches.

Is Gobbly.io good for travel?

Yes: Yes. Every level works without Wi-Fi or mobile data. Ads, purchases and cloud saving need the internet. Levels take about 1 to 3 minutes, and the download is about 90 MB.
