What is Gobbly.io?

Last updated 2026-11-17

Gobbly.io is a free casual game for iPhone and Android where you roll a hungry little ball around colourful maps, eat the goal items before the timer runs out, and grow bigger with every bite. You start each level as a small ball that can only eat little things, and every bite helps you grow until you can eat cakes, chairs and cars. It is free on the App Store and Google Play.

What do you actually do in the game?

The game is made of levels. Each level shows a few goal cards at the top, such as 120 cookies, 30 donuts and 4 burgers. Eat every goal item before the timer reaches zero and you win the level. There are hundreds of levels, with HARD and SUPER HARD levels along the way.

The ball starts every level at Size 1. Everything you eat fills the ring around it, and when the ring is full the ball grows to the next size and can eat bigger things.

What kind of game is it?

  • Eat-and-grow game: the same idea as hole games and the classic rolling-ball games: get bigger by swallowing what is around you.
  • Level game: hundreds of short levels, each with its own goal list and timer, that get trickier in waves.
  • Casual game: one finger, learned in seconds, a level fits into a short break.

Why a ball instead of a hole?

A hole sits flat on the ground and things drop into it. A ball rolls through the world, bumps into things and grows bigger on screen. Tall piles of food slide down into it as you eat the bottom, which makes eating feel physical.

What else is in the game?

Boosters help when a level is tight: Bigger Ball, Magnet, Shield, Compass, Freeze, Rainbow Potion and Clock. You earn them as rewards and can buy them with coins. Winning levels earns coins and stars. On top of the levels there are daily gifts, a prize road, win-streak gifts, the Super Star power, Critter Hunts, a Weekly Contest, a monthly Season Pass, a card album and a collection book of everything you have eaten.

Who made it?

Gobbly.io is made by Sultan of Niyyat, an independent developer. It is not made by, or connected to, the companies behind Hole.io, Katamari Damacy or other games in the genre.

Where can you get it?

Only from the official stores: Gobbly.io on the App Store and Gobbly.io on Google Play.
