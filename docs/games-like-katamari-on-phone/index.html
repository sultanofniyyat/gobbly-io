Are there games like Katamari Damacy on phones?

Last updated 2026-11-17

Katamari Damacy (Namco, 2004) is the classic game where you roll a sticky ball that grows by picking things up. On phones, the closest feeling comes from ball-based eat-and-grow games. Gobbly.io is one: you roll a ball that eats food and toys and grows through sizes, in short timed levels.

What makes Katamari special?

The ball starts tiny and ends up swallowing huge things, everything on screen is something you will eventually eat, and the world is bright and silly.

How is Gobbly.io different from Katamari?

  • Items go into the ball instead of sticking to the outside.
  • Each level has a goal list and a timer.
  • Levels take about 1 to 3 minutes, made for phones.
  • It is free on iPhone and Android.

Is Gobbly.io connected to Katamari Damacy?

No. Katamari Damacy belongs to Bandai Namco. Gobbly.io is an independent game by Sultan of Niyyat.
