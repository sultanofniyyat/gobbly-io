Will I lose my progress in Gobbly.io?

Last updated 2026-11-17

Your progress is saved on your phone automatically. On Android it is also saved in the cloud with Google Play Games, so it comes back on a new phone signed in to the same Google account. On iPhone there is no cloud save yet, so deleting the app deletes your progress. Remove Ads comes back on any phone with Restore Purchases.

Is progress saved automatically?

Yes. You never need to press save.

What happens if I update the game?

Updates keep your progress. Always update from the App Store or Google Play.

How do I move my progress to a new phone?

On Android, sign in to Google Play Games with the same Google account on the new phone and your progress comes back. On iPhone this is not possible yet. On both, Remove Ads comes back with Restore purchases in the game's Settings.

My progress disappeared. What can I do?

Check that the game was not deleted and that the phone's storage was not cleared. On Android, check you are signed in to the same Google Play Games account. If something still looks wrong, email sultanofniyyat@gmail.com with your phone model and what happened.
