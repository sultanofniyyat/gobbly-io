What are fun games where you eat food?

Last updated 2026-11-17

Food games range from cooking and restaurant games to eating games where your character gobbles everything in sight. Gobbly.io is the eating kind: Gobbly, a hungry ball, eats fruit, cookies, donuts, burgers, cakes and more, and grows with every bite.

What kinds of food games are there?

  • Cooking and restaurant: make and serve food on time.
  • Food sorting and matching: line up or sort snacks.
  • Eating games: eat as much as possible, often to grow.

What do you eat in Gobbly.io?

You start with small things like cherries and cookies, then donuts and sandwiches, then burgers and cakes, and on the biggest sizes even chairs and cars. Each level lists the foods you must eat to win.

Is it a cooking game?

No. There is no cooking or serving: it is all about eating and growing.
