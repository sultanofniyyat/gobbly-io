What makes a good puzzle-arcade game with levels?

Last updated 2026-11-17

Good level-based puzzle-arcade games start easy, get harder in waves, give a clear goal every level and let you win without paying. Gobbly.io mixes arcade movement (rolling and growing) with a puzzle side: choosing what to eat first so you are big enough for the last goal items.

What should I look for?

  • Fair difficulty: hard levels now and then, followed by easier ones.
  • A clear goal on every level.
  • Boosters you can earn, not only buy.
  • No wall that makes you pay to continue.

How does Gobbly.io handle difficulty?

Levels come in waves, with HARD and SUPER HARD levels along the way that give bigger rewards. Every level can be beaten without buying anything. Boosters help when a level is tight: Bigger Ball, Magnet, Shield, Compass, Freeze, Rainbow Potion and Clock. You earn them as rewards and can buy them with coins.
