How long is a level, and is it good for short breaks?

Last updated 2026-11-17

A level of Gobbly.io takes about 1 to 3 minutes. Early levels are short and later levels get longer. That makes it a good fit for short breaks, commutes and waiting rooms.

Can I pause in the middle of a level?

Yes, with the pause button at the top right. Quitting a level from the pause screen, or closing the app in the middle of a level, counts as giving up and costs one heart.

How do hearts work?

You have 5 hearts. Losing a level costs one heart, and one heart comes back every 30 minutes. You can also refill them with coins, or watch a short video for one heart. Winning never costs a heart, so you can keep playing as long as you keep winning.

Do I lose progress if I stop?

No. Your level, coins and rewards are kept. Your progress is saved on your phone automatically. On Android it is also saved in the cloud with Google Play Games, so it comes back on a new phone signed in to the same Google account. On iPhone there is no cloud save yet, so deleting the app deletes your progress. Remove Ads comes back on any phone with Restore Purchases.

Is it good for the commute?

Yes: one-finger controls work while you hold the phone in one hand. Yes. Every level works without Wi-Fi or mobile data. Ads, purchases and cloud saving need the internet.
