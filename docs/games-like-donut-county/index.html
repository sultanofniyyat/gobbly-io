What can I play after Donut County?

Last updated 2026-11-17

Donut County (2018) is a short, funny story game about a hole that swallows a town. If you want more of that swallowing, look at other eat-and-grow games: Hole.io for online rounds, Katamari Damacy for the rolling-ball classic, or Gobbly.io for hundreds of short levels with a growing ball.

What do people miss after Donut County?

The swallowing itself, the cute world and the "one more thing" feeling. Donut County is short, so many players finish it in an evening and want more.

What does Gobbly.io offer instead?

  • Hundreds of levels instead of a short story.
  • A ball instead of a hole.
  • Colourful food and toy maps.
  • Free to play, on iPhone and Android.

It has no story, so if the writing was your favourite part of Donut County, that is the one thing it will not replace.
