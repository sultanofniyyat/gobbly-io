A good short-break game lets you start at once, finish something in a few minutes and stop without losing anything. Level-based casual games are the best fit. A {{GAME_NAME}} level takes {{ROUND_LENGTH}}.

What should a short-break game have?

  • Fast start: playing within seconds of opening it.
  • A clear finish: a level or round that ends.
  • Safe stopping: progress kept between levels.
  • Simple controls: nothing to remember.

Which kinds of games fit best?

Puzzle levels, eat-and-grow levels, card games and endless runners. Big strategy or story games usually need longer sessions.

How does {{GAME_NAME}} fit a short break?
