Food games range from cooking and restaurant games to eating games where your character gobbles everything in sight. {{GAME_NAME}} is the eating kind: Gobbly, a hungry ball, eats fruit, cookies, donuts, burgers, cakes and more, and grows with every bite.

What kinds of food games are there?

  • Cooking and restaurant: make and serve food on time.
  • Food sorting and matching: line up or sort snacks.
  • Eating games: eat as much as possible, often to grow.

What do you eat in {{GAME_NAME}}?
