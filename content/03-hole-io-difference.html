It is the same family of game: you swallow things, grow bigger and can then swallow bigger things. The differences are that in {{GAME_NAME}} you are a rolling ball, not a hole, and you play single-player levels with a goal list and a timer instead of racing other players to be the biggest.

What is the same?

  • You start small and grow by eating things.
  • The bigger you are, the bigger the things you can eat.
  • There is a timer, and one finger controls everything.

Is it a copy of Hole.io?

No. Hole.io made the hole version of this idea popular on phones, and the rolling-ball idea goes back to Katamari Damacy on PlayStation 2 in 2004. {{GAME_NAME}} is an independent game by {{STUDIO}} with its own maps, food and levels, and it is not connected to either.

If I like Hole.io, will I like this?

Very likely, if what you enjoy is the growing and the moment you are finally big enough for the big things. If you mainly enjoy beating other players, note that {{GAME_NAME}} is about beating the clock instead.
