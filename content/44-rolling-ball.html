Rolling ball games come in a few kinds: tilt mazes, downhill runners, physics puzzles and eat-and-grow games. {{GAME_NAME}} is an eat-and-grow ball game: you drag to roll a ball that eats food and toys and grows through sizes.

What are the main kinds?

KindWhat you do
Tilt mazeTilt the phone to guide a ball to a hole
RunnerSteer a ball down a track without falling off
Physics puzzleBuild or move parts so the ball reaches the goal
Eat and growRoll around, swallow things, get bigger ({{GAME_NAME}})

How does the ball move in {{GAME_NAME}}?
