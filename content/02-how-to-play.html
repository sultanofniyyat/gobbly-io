{{CONTROLS_SENTENCE}} Roll into food and things that are small enough and the ball eats them. Eat every item on the goal cards before the timer runs out to win the level.

How do the controls work?

{{CONTROLS_SENTENCE}} There are no buttons to learn in the play area, only the booster bar at the bottom and the pause button.

What are the goal cards?

The cards under the timer show what you must eat and how many are left. Each one counts down as you eat. When every card is done, the level is won at once, and seconds left on the clock give you a small coin bonus.

How do you grow?

{{SIZES_SENTENCE}} Small things are worth a little, bigger things are worth more. A "Size" label under the ball shows how big you are.

What happens if the time runs out?

An "OUT OF TIME" screen shows what you still had to eat. You can buy 20 more seconds with coins, watch a short video for more time, or give up. Giving up costs one heart.

Is it hard to learn?

No. The first level teaches itself without any text. Getting good is about choosing a route: eat the small things first, grow, then go for the big goal items.
