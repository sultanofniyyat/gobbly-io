Long description

Meet Gobbly, a hungry little ball with a giant appetite. Roll around colourful maps and gobble up fruit, snacks, toys and more. Every bite makes you bigger, and the bigger you get, the bigger the things you can eat. Each level gives you a goal list and a clock; tall piles of food pour into the ball, bombs keep you careful, and boosters, a Season Pass, Critter Hunts and a Weekly Contest keep it growing. Free on iPhone and Android, one finger, playable offline.
