Eat as many small things as you can in the first seconds, roll through crowded areas instead of chasing single items, and move up to bigger things as soon as you grow. Keep moving: empty ground wastes time.

What are the best tips to grow fast?

  1. Go for crowds. A line or pile of small food is worth more than single items spread across the map.
  2. Keep rolling. The ball eats as it rolls past. Rolling through a full area beats stopping on one item.
  3. Park next to piles. Tall stacks pour into the ball by themselves while you sit at their base.
  4. Move up a size as soon as you can. Bigger things fill the size ring much faster.
  5. Do not go back to cleared ground. Head for places you have not been.
  6. Use Magnet when food is spread out. It pulls things in from farther away.
  7. Start with Bigger Ball or Rainbow Potion on hard levels. Rainbow Potion starts you at Size 2.
  8. Watch out for bombs. One wrong bite ends the level, so slow down near them or use a Shield.

Is it better to eat small or big things?

Both, at the right time. Small things early, because they are all you can eat. Big things as soon as you can, because each one is worth much more. The mistake is staying on small things after you have outgrown them.
