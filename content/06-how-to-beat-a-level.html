Read the goal cards first, grow quickly on the small food, and only then go for the big goal items that need a high size. Use boosters on HARD and SUPER HARD levels, and keep away from bombs. Every level can be beaten without buying anything.

How should I read the goal cards?

Most levels mix "many small" goals (lots of cookies), "some medium" goals and "a few big" goals that you can only eat after growing. Eat the small goal items as you grow, and save the big ones for when you are big enough.

Which boosters help most?

  • Clock and Freeze: more time, or the timer stops for a while.
  • Magnet: pulls food in from farther away.
  • Compass: points to the goal items you still need.
  • Shield: protects you from a bomb.
  • Bigger Ball and Rainbow Potion: a head start on size.

What do I do when time is nearly out?

Go straight for the goal items that are closest. When the cards with only a few items left start to pulse, that is the game telling you what is left. If time runs out, you can buy 20 more seconds with coins or watch a video for more time.

What are HARD and SUPER HARD levels?

Every few levels the game gets tougher for one level, with a tighter clock or more to eat. They give bigger rewards. This is a good moment to use the boosters you have saved.
