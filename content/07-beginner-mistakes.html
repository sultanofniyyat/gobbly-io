The big ones are going for the big goal items before you are big enough, stopping on single items, going back over cleared ground, and rolling into bombs. Fix those and levels get much easier.

Which mistakes do new players make most?

  1. Going for big goals too early. You bounce off and lose time. Fix: grow on small food first.
  2. Stopping on single items. Fix: roll through crowds of food.
  3. Going back over cleared ground. Fix: always head for places you have not been.
  4. Rolling into bombs. Fix: slow down near them, or use a Shield.
  5. Saving boosters forever. Fix: use them on HARD levels; you earn more as rewards.
  6. Giving up too fast. Giving up costs a heart. Fix: if you were close, take the extra time.

How long does it take to get good?

Not long. The first levels teach the basics, and most players get the hang of routes and sizes within a few levels.
