Why is it called Gobbly.io then?

The ".io" is a nod to the family of eat-and-grow games it belongs to. The game itself is played in single-player levels, with a goal list and a clock instead of opponents.

How do the Weekly Contest and rankings work?

They give you scores to beat and prizes for playing well. They are worked out on your phone and nothing about you is shown to other people.

Can I play with friends?

Not in the same level. Many players compare how far they have got.
