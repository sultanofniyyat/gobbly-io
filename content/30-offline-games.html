What does "offline" really mean in a free game?

Many games say "offline" but still need the internet for part of the game. A truly offline game lets you start, play and finish levels in airplane mode. Usually only extras need a connection: ads, purchases and cloud saving.

Which kinds of games work best offline?

  • Level-based puzzle and arcade games: the levels are already on your phone.
  • Endless runners and score chasers: you play against your own best score.
  • Story and adventure games you buy once: such as Monument Valley or Stardew Valley.

Games built on live matches against other people, like most io games, need the internet.

How do I check if a game works offline before a trip?

  1. Install and open it once at home, on Wi-Fi.
  2. Switch on airplane mode and start a level.
  3. If it plays, it will work on the plane or the train.
