Eat-and-grow games are games where you swallow things to get bigger, and getting bigger lets you swallow bigger things. You might be a hole, a ball, a cell or a snake. {{GAME_NAME}} is the ball kind: you roll around maps eating food and toys before the timer runs out.

What are the main styles?

  • Hole: things fall into a hole that grows (Hole.io, Donut County).
  • Ball: a ball rolls and takes things in (Katamari Damacy, {{GAME_NAME}}).
  • Cell: eat smaller cells and pellets (Agar.io).
  • Snake: eat to grow longer (Slither.io).

Why are they so popular on phones?

The idea is understood in seconds, the controls fit one finger, and growth is easy to see. Every level has a natural build-up: tiny at the start, huge at the end.

What does {{GAME_NAME}} add?

A goal list on every level, so you know exactly what to eat; sizes that unlock bigger food; piles that pour into the ball; bombs to avoid; and boosters for tight levels.
