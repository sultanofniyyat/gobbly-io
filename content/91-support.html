Something not working? Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}. We read every message.

What to include

  • Your phone model and its iOS or Android version
  • What happened, and what you expected to happen
  • A screenshot or short screen recording if you can
  • For purchase problems: the date, and on Android the order number (it starts with GPA.)

Quick answers

  • Paid for Remove Ads but still see ads
  • Lost progress or changing phones
  • Phone requirements and safe download
  • Playing without internet
  • Questions from parents

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple or Google, and refunds are asked for there. iPhone: reportaproblem.apple.com. Android: play.google.com → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history, then choose the order. If that does not work, email us.
