What makes it different

Most "eat and grow" phone games use a hole in the ground that things fall into. In {{GAME_NAME}} you are a ball. It rolls, it has weight, and big piles of food tumble down into it as you eat them from the bottom. Every level gives you a clear shopping list and a clock, so there is always a goal: "just 4 burgers left".
