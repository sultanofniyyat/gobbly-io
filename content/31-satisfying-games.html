"Satisfying" games give you lots of small, visible results: a mess becomes tidy, things fit perfectly, or something grows with every touch. The main kinds are clean-up games, sorting games, eat-and-grow games and slicing or crushing games. {{GAME_NAME}} is an eat-and-grow game: piles of food pour into your ball and a full map ends up empty.

What kinds of satisfying games are there?

KindThe feeling
Clean-upWashing, scraping or tidying until it shines
SortingColours, balls or items ending up in the right place
Eat and growSwallowing everything and getting bigger (hole games, {{GAME_NAME}})
Slicing and crushingCutting or pressing things with a clean result
Stacking and fittingThings that slot together exactly

Why do these games feel so good?

They give a steady stream of tiny rewards you can see and hear, and a clear finish. Short rounds mean you get that finish often.

Which kind should I try?

If you like calm and slow, try sorting or clean-up games. If you like motion and growth, try eat-and-grow games. {{GAME_NAME}} adds a clock and a goal list, so there is a "just 3 burgers left!" moment in every level.
