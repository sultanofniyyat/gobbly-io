Hyper-casual games are tiny, instant games with one simple action and almost nothing else. Hybrid-casual games keep that simple action but add progress to come back for: levels, boosters, collections and events. {{GAME_NAME}} is hybrid-casual.

What is a hyper-casual game?

One mechanic, learned in a second, short rounds, little or no progress system. Many early io games and "tap to stack" games are hyper-casual.

What is a hybrid-casual game?

The same easy core, plus a meta layer: hundreds of levels, upgrades or boosters, daily rewards, seasons and collections. The game is still easy to start, but there is more to work towards.

Why is {{GAME_NAME}} hybrid-casual?
