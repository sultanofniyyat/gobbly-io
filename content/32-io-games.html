The best-known io games are Agar.io (eat cells to grow), Slither.io (a growing snake), Paper.io (claim territory) and Hole.io (a hole that swallows a city). They share one-finger controls and short rounds. Most of them match you against other players online.

What is each famous io game like?

GameWhat you do
Agar.ioA cell that eats pellets and smaller cells
Slither.ioA snake that grows longer and traps others
Paper.ioDraw loops to claim more of the board
Hole.ioA hole that swallows people, cars and buildings

How do I choose one?

  • Want to compete with real people? Pick one with live online matches.
  • Want growth without the stress of other players? Pick a single-player eat-and-grow game.
  • Playing on the move? Check whether it works without internet.
